Job Description: Senior AI Engineer — Talent Matching
Company: Northwind Talent Labs (fictional company; sample JD written for the TalentSignal project)
Location: Pune or Noida, India (Hybrid — three office days a week) | Engineers willing to relocate are welcome
Employment Type: Full-time
Experience: 5–9 years

About this sample
This job description is original text written for TalentSignal's tests, demos and documentation. It describes a realistic search-and-matching role so that every part of the job-description parser is exercised.

What you will do
- Own the retrieval layer that decides which candidates a recruiter sees first for a new opening.
- Turn vague hiring briefs into measurable relevance targets, then ship models that move them.
- Keep the matching service fast and reliable as the candidate pool grows tenfold.
- Review designs, mentor two engineers and write a meaningful share of the code yourself.

Must-have qualifications
- Production experience building semantic search with text embeddings (for example sentence-transformers or BGE models) for real users.
- Hands-on work with a vector database or ANN index such as FAISS, Qdrant or OpenSearch, including hybrid search that mixes lexical and dense scores.
- Strong Python; you write clear, tested code and review other people's code carefully.
- Rigorous offline evaluation of rankings with NDCG or MRR, and experience running A/B tests to confirm offline gains online.

Nice to have
- Fine-tuning language models with LoRA or QLoRA for a domain-specific task.
- Learning-to-rank experience with LambdaMART or LightGBM rankers.
- Open source contributions to search, NLP or ML tooling.

The ideal candidate
Someone with 6-8 years in software, of which 4 or more years in applied machine learning, who has taken at least one ranking system from prototype to production and can explain the trade-offs they made.

Who is not a fit
- Careers spent entirely at consulting firms on short client engagements, with no product ownership.
- Pure research roles where nothing was ever deployed to users.
- Engineers who have not written production code for several years after moving into architecture.
- A pattern of switching companies every year mainly to collect bigger titles.
- Experience limited to computer vision, speech or robotics, with no text retrieval work.

Logistics
- Notice period of 30 days or less is preferred; we can buy out up to 30 days.
- We cannot sponsor work visas for this role.

Final note
Please don't optimise your application for keyword matching. Listing Pinecone, RAG or the latest framework is not what we look for; show us the systems you built and how you knew they worked.
